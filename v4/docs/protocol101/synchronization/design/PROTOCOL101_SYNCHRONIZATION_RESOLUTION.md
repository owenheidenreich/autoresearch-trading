# Protocol101 Synchronization Resolution

## Objective

The project must select one causal market-data and feature contract that can be
used in historical validation, captured-input replay, live shadow operation,
and guarded IBKR paper execution.

The target is equivalent causal decision behavior. Raw IBKR and Databento
prices are not expected to be byte-identical.

## Baseline Correction

The older `359 trades / $136,800` headline combines two separately reported
scopes:

```text
Q1 2026:    249 trades / $95,010
March 2026: 110 trades / $41,790
combined:   359 trades / $136,800
```

It is not a clean 60-session Q1-only benchmark. The event-policy evaluation
also consumes precomputed candidate exit times and candidate PnL. It may remain
useful as research history, but it is not a live-relevant equity benchmark.

The provisional apples-to-apples benchmark is the current serial runner under
the historical feature contract:

```text
same-runner legacy: 221 trades / $69,180
protocol101-live-v1: 151 trades / $17,480
diagnostic context-lag-0: 191 trades / $48,230
fair timing context-lag-0 decision+1: 200 trades / -$14,230
```

## Fair-Game Policy

The project direction is now:

1. Keep strict one-account serial replay. Do not go back to overlapping or
   precomputed-path headline metrics.
2. Keep live-reproducibility as the law for model-facing features.
3. Repair live-reproducible feature semantics only where causality can be
   proven from available live inputs before the decision.
4. Accept lost edge when it depended on unavailable, stale, retrospective, or
   non-causal information.
5. If frozen Protocol101 cannot perform well under the final fair contract,
   retrain on that fair contract instead of tuning around the mismatch.

This means the first degradation, from event-policy/precomputed-path evaluation
to strict one-account serial replay, is an accepted trustworthiness correction.
It removed simulation privilege and made the result less flattering but more
live-relevant.

The second degradation, from same-runner legacy to `protocol101-live-v1`, is the
active synchronization problem. Some of it may be necessary because the old
feature semantics are not available live. Some of it may be needless, as the
diagnostic context-lag-0 rebuild showed by recovering material Q1 behavior
without changing model weights or thresholds.

However, the fair timing check changed the conclusion. A live-plausible
completed-minute contract can observe minute T only after that minute is
complete and can then enter on minute T+1. When the diagnostic context-lag-0
source policy was forced through that rule, Q1 fell to `200` trades and
`-$14,230`. That rejects same-minute completed-bar recovery as a production
repair. It remains a useful forensic clue about where the old model found
signal, but it is not a fair live contract.

The goal is not to recover the old edge. The goal is to preserve causal edge,
remove fake edge, and avoid throwing away live-reproducible edge unnecessarily.

## Lifecycle Repair

The captured-input lifecycle replay previously supplied Protocol066 with one
isolated row per minute even though the frozen model requires the complete
causal path since entry. It also stopped canonical rows at 15:30 ET, making a
15:55 ET forced-flat impossible.

The repaired replay:

- Builds entry decisions through 15:30 ET.
- Preserves lifecycle quote rows through 15:55 ET.
- Feeds Protocol066 the full causal sequence on every lifecycle step.
- Computes rolling PnL path features causally.
- Emits a terminal forced-flat at 15:55 ET when still open.
- Flags a stale terminal quote when a historical capture lost the contract.
- Records step and sequence hashes for reconstruction.

The recorder now retains prior ladder subscriptions within a bounded
market-data-line cap so future lifecycle paths are less likely to disappear as
ATM moves.

These are replay and acquisition correctness repairs. They do not change model
weights or thresholds.

## Q1 Feature Evidence

The repaired multi-session audit pairs all `21,960` Q1 decision minutes.
Candidate identity overlaps are usually high, but ranking and score behavior
are not equivalent:

- `1,428` action mismatches.
- Median absolute max-edge drift is about `10.41` points.
- Top-contract match rate is about `31.6%`.
- Option volume and open interest are among the largest standardized drifts.
- Several OMAR, breakout-pattern, and momentum features also show material
  standardized drift.

Frozen-model counterfactual rescoring makes the direction clearer:

- Replacing the live-v1 pattern-context group with legacy values closes about
  `10.08` edge points on average.
- Replacing all token features closes about `13.59` edge points on average.
- Quote/spread replacement closes about `0.82` edge points on average.
- Option-volume replacement closes about `0.03` edge points on average.
- Open-interest replacement slightly worsens average closure by about `0.12`
  edge points.

These counterfactual effects are non-additive and are score attribution, not
standalone PnL claims. They strongly suggest that the dominant Q1 degradation
is in derived pattern/context semantics rather than a simple absence of
volume/open interest or an IBKR subscription setting.

## Data-Plane Candidates

### Enriched IBKR

The recorder now requests generic ticks `100,101` and preserves option daily
volume and open-interest diagnostics. These values are not treated as
equivalent to Databento one-minute OHLCV or official statistics.

This candidate remains unselected until feature ablations and prospective
parity evidence show that it preserves the frozen model's causal feature and
score distributions.

### Same-Vendor Model Feed

Databento live/historical OPRA plus ThetaData live/historical SPX/VIX remains a
candidate model-facing data plane. IBKR would remain the account, order, fill,
and execution-guard plane.

No subscription has been purchased or changed. A portal quote and evidence
review are required first.

## Current Gates

The current `protocol101-live-v1` Q1 result fails the historical
non-inferiority gate. Data-plane selection is therefore `evidence_pending`,
paper readiness is `blocked`, and retraining is not yet authorized.

The diagnostic context-lag-0 source policy recovered Q1 from `151` trades /
`$17,480` to `191` trades / `$48,230`, but it still failed non-inferiority
against same-runner legacy because it recovered only about `69.7%` of legacy
P&L. More importantly, the fair timing version that waits until the completed
minute is actually knowable produced `200` trades and `-$14,230`. This turns
the diagnostic into a rejected production repair route, not a candidate
contract mutation.

The July 2 paired replay is now strong single-day development evidence:
captured-input replay is exact, the nine IBKR threshold-crossing entries match
the same historical entry timestamps/contracts, and the repaired lifecycle
paths match the historical serial exits by timestamp and reason. This proves
the repaired replay machinery can synchronize a useful day. It does not prove
that the broader Q1 feature-distribution problem is solved.

The Q1 trade/PnL attribution now shows the main degradation is not a missing
strike-ladder problem. Lost legacy trades still have high candidate identity
overlap, but score/ranking and feature semantics suppress entries. The largest
repair target is canonical pattern/index/structure semantics. Open interest and
volume remain diagnostic/enrichment candidates, but they are not automatic
replacements for Databento historical fields.

Same-input regression fixtures now cover both structure features and
market-window features. Both match when live and historical are fed identical
canonical minutes. Therefore, the remaining Q1 degradation should be treated as
feature-contract/data-policy shift unless future evidence proves a narrower
formula bug.

The fair-contract training preflight now inspects the existing
`protocol101-live-v1` Q1 processed and normalized datasets directly. It confirms
`21,960` processed rows across `61` exact sessions, all marked
`protocol101-live-v1`, with no future quote/context rows and a median
context lag of one minute. It also found a real cleanup blocker: the processed
directory contains `62` pickle files because `2026-01-02 2.pkl` duplicates the
exact `2026-01-02.pkl` session. The duplicate is not byte-identical and has
zero label targets, while the canonical exact-date file has three label
targets. This duplicate must be archived, removed, or excluded by a required
manifest before any training runner is allowed to glob the dataset.

The duplicate has now been archived under the fair-contract training preflight
audit directory with its original hash/stat evidence preserved. The preflight
now passes with `61` canonical sessions, `21,960` processed rows, all marked
`protocol101-live-v1`, and no future quote/context rows. A manifest-based
training design packet exists, but it does not authorize model training,
threshold tuning, paper-submit, or a paper-default change.

The next decisive evidence is:

1. Use the generated data-plane decision map to audit the highest-impact
   feature families without tuning weights or thresholds.
2. Reject same-minute completed-bar recovery unless a genuinely intra-minute
   causal model/feed contract is designed and separately validated.
3. Require future fair-contract training runners to load the generated
   canonical manifest instead of globbing the processed directory.
4. Obtain explicit owner approval before any model training on the
   `protocol101-live-v1` manifest.
5. Run the July 6-10 recorder-first packet to rule out July 2 being a lucky
   narrow case and to gather more call/put, time-bucket, and lifecycle paths.
6. Evaluate enriched IBKR and same-vendor feeds without tuning the frozen
   threshold.
7. Select the causal contract or formally declare that retraining is required.

## Safety State

- Protocol101 remains the paper default.
- Paper-submit runtime authorization is disabled until
  `Protocol101PaperReadinessGateV2` passes and the owner separately approves
  execution calibration.
- Real-money trading remains disabled.
- No broker orders are part of synchronization replay.
- No model training or threshold tuning is part of this phase.
- Paid downloads remain limited to explicitly approved dates and caps.
