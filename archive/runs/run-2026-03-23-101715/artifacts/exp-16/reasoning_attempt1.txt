The key insight is that magnitude weighting in gate loss may be creating a perverse incentive: large losses get stronger "don't trade" signals than small wins get "do trade" signals. I'll remove magnitude weighting entirely and add a simple trade frequency bonus directly in the loss function to reward the model for actually making trades, breaking the zero-trade attractor.
