I'll switch from EV-weighted sigmoid gate targeting to a simpler binary classification approach. Instead of using sigmoid(best_pnl * scale) as soft targets, I'll use hard binary targets: gate_target=1 when best_pnl > threshold, 0 otherwise. This eliminates the "don't trade" attractor while keeping selectivity. I'll also increase the profitability threshold from 0.0 to 0.02 (2% minimum return) to improve trade quality and reduce the magnitude weight cap to prevent gradient domination by large losses.
