The single biggest weakness appears to be training instability - we've had two runs with the same hyperparameters (WEIGHT_DECAY=0.08, LR=1.2e-4) produce drastically different results (score=-1.13 vs score=1.63), and experiment #3 collapsed completely with 0 trades. This suggests the model is sensitive to initialization or early training dynamics. The approach I haven't tried yet is adding regularization through feature noise injection during training, which can improve generalization and reduce overfitting to specific market patterns. I believe adding FEATURE_NOISE_STD=0.08 alongside WEIGHT_DECAY=0.07 will stabilize training by preventing the model from memorizing exact feature patterns, leading to more consistent performance across different market regimes.
