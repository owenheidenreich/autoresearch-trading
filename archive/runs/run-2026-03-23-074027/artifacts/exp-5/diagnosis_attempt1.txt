Looking at the recent experiments, there's a clear pattern of catastrophic failure (score=-10.0) when trying basic regularization approaches like higher dropout, reduced model capacity, or stronger direction signals. All these changes triggered critical anomalies including "cost_realism_low_coverage" and "entry_quality_too_low", suggesting the model completely stopped making reasonable trades. The current best experiment (#5) has a negative score (-0.27) with PF=0.89 and only 1.0 trades per day, indicating the model is barely trading and when it does, it's slightly unprofitable. The single biggest weakness is that the model is too conservative - it's learned to mostly avoid trading rather than learning to trade well. I haven't tried making the gate head MORE aggressive (encouraging more trading) while simultaneously improving trade quality through bias initialization that favors high-probability setups.
