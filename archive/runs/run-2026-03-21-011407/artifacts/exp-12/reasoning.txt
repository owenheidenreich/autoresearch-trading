The current model shows sharp performance degradation over time - early validation chunks have PF >5 while late chunks have PF <1. This suggests regime overfitting where the model learned patterns from high-volatility periods that don't generalize. The model compensates for shrinking winners by increasing trade frequency (2→4 trades/day), making things worse. I hypothesize that adding temporal consistency regularization during training will help the model learn more robust patterns that work across different market regimes.
