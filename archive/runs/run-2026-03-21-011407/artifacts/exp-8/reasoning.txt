The current best model shows strong performance degradation over time: early validation chunks have PF >5 while late chunks have PF <1. This suggests the model is overfit to earlier market regimes with higher volatility where large OTM moves produced outsized returns. The winners shrink from ~120% to ~27% across the backtest while the model compensates by trading MORE (2→4 trades/day), making things worse. I'll implement temporal consistency regularization by adding a penalty term that penalizes large differences in performance across time chunks during training, forcing the model to learn more robust patterns.
