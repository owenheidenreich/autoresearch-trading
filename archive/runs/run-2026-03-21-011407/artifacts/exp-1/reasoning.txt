The current best model (exp-52) shows strong aggregate performance but suffers from severe regime degradation - early validation chunks have PF >5 while late chunks drop to PF <1. The model trains to ~120% average winners early but degrades to ~27% winners over time while maintaining ~-18% losers. This suggests overfitting to a high-volatility regime where large OTM moves were profitable. I hypothesize that reducing the OTM bias (especially OTM5 PUT bias of +0.35) and strengthening ATM preference will create more regime-robust performance, as ATM options have more consistent gamma exposure across volatility regimes.
