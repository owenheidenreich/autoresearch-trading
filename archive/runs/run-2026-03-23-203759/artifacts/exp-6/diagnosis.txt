The model is performing well with PF=2.3 and score=3.88, but several recent experiments failed catastrophically (score=-10, zero trades). The pattern shows that increasing EXIT_LOSS_WEIGHT to 0.8 and reducing GATE_LOSS_WEIGHT to 0.3 both caused zero-trade collapse. The successful experiment #2 only changed DIR_LOSS_WEIGHT from 1.0 to 2.0, which improved direction selection while keeping the gate balanced. The biggest weakness appears to be inconsistent training dynamics when loss weights are imbalanced - the gate head needs sufficient weight to learn when to trade, but the direction head also needs emphasis to pick profitable strikes.
