The biggest weakness appears to be the over-regularization from multiple consistency loss terms. The current best has TEMPORAL_CHUNK_WEIGHT=0.5 and CROSS_TEMPORAL_CONSISTENCY_WEIGHT=0.3, which together create 0.8 units of consistency penalty versus only 1.0+1.0=2.0 units of core gate+direction loss. This heavy regularization may be suppressing the model's ability to learn profitable patterns. Additionally, looking at the trajectory, all recent experiments that failed had various combinations of consistency weights but none tried reducing the regularization burden while keeping the proven MAXIMUM_CONSERVATIVE_GATE_BIAS that delivered the big score jump from 3.2→8.2.
