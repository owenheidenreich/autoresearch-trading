## Phase 1 design: two questions, not one blended experiment

Phase 1 should not mix ES direction, SPXW execution, and learned exit timing.

### Phase 1A — causal direction mechanism

Purpose: decide whether a named causal setup predicts a fixed, independently chosen directional horizon on owned, non-reserved development data.

Before outcomes:

1. Owner selects the game: **ES directional precursor** (recommended first) or **executable SPXW economics**. Passing the former is not proof of the latter.

5. Run a session-clustered known-answer campaign at the owner-signed effect with at least 80% recovery and clean family-corrected nulls. If the effect is not measurable, stop before real outcomes.

## Proposed economic bar — awaiting owner signature

No signed entry “trader's bar” exists. The following proposal deliberately separates economic usefulness from evidence strength.

### Economic usefulness for an executable SPXW candidate

- Mean net P&L **at least +$25 per executed ticket**, after exact ask/bid, fees, declared stop and slippage.
- Mean net P&L **at least +$10 per eligible session**, counting every no-trigger day as $0. This makes the policy worth about $2,520/year before compounding at 252 sessions and prevents one spectacular rare trade from satisfying the programme goal.
- Paired lift over the preregistered causal opposite-direction/control policy is positive on the same session index.
- Point-estimate net P&L and paired lift have the correct sign in at least four of five chronological blocks.
- The serial-account path passes the signed breaker/equity-floor law; report worst realised loss, maximum drawdown, surviving-path per-ticket economics, ruin probability, and time-to-ruin as headlines.
- Hit rate and win/loss ratio are diagnostics, never substitutes for realised expectancy. A hypothetical 40%/3:1 payoff is relevant only if exact fills produce it.

### Evidence strength

- The family, controls, statistic, multiplicity, session cluster, exclusions, stopping rule, and verdict are frozen first.
- A known-answer campaign recovers the **same owner-approved economic effect** at least 80% of the time with clean null behavior before outcomes open.
- The development result is labeled `CANDIDATE`, even if it clears the point bar. Independent forward confirmation remains necessary.
- If the known-answer campaign is underpowered, do not weaken the bar after seeing that fact and do not peek.

`+$25/ticket` and `+$10/eligible session` are Tier-2 recommendations, not signed inheritance. If the owner chooses an ES-only precursor, it needs a separate directional effect and translation law; the SPXW dollar bar must not be back-converted from the $17.92 ES round trip.

## Occupancy law

Use three names consistently:

1. **Opportunity occupancy:** all causal moments the rule could inspect. Useful for mechanism diagnostics only.
2. **Trigger occupancy:** moments the frozen rule says qualify. Multiple triggers in one day remain one statistical cluster.
3. **Executable occupancy:** actual tickets after the frozen first-two/risk selector. This is capped at two under current law and is the only occupancy that may support policy P&L.

The executable selector must be causal and frozen before outcomes. If it ranks triggers, that ranker is part of the declared strategy; “best two after the fact” is an oracle. No amendment to the risk law should be made merely to make a power calculation fit. If the owner changes product, ticket count, sizing, stop, or breaker, the entire risk and known-answer preflight must be re-derived first.

## Family decision

There is **no evidenced best runnable family today**.

- Opening-range/overnight-gap M1/M3 remain unfalsified, but unchanged reruns are closed. M1/M3 were already roughly 56%/99% occupied; calling them rare or high-occupancy does not create a new design. Any new horizon, trigger, abstention, or within-session formulation needs its own declared family size, session-clustered MDE, and known-answer campaign.
- Strict individual option prints signed against a causally prior touch plus post-print non-replenishment are the only materially new observable identified by the latest review. They are the best **scientific preflight question**, not a route recommendation. Current blockers are tied-clock order ambiguity, only 229 session clusters, missing broad data/live parity/runtime/fill law, and inadequate target-specific power; the current verdict stays `STOP`.
- Unconditional chart/clock/geometry drift, the exact fitted entry configuration, the exact post-hoc level gate, and the exact exit configuration stay closed.

Family selection may use outcome-blind availability, causal identifiability, parity, occupancy, and known-answer power. It may not inspect economics to choose which family receives the one outcome opening.
