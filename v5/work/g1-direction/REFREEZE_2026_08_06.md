# G1 family re-freeze, 2026-08-06

**One sentence: the frozen declaration was reissued because the machine's home directory was renamed
underneath it, and nothing about the experiment changed.**

The G1 family was frozen earlier on 2026-08-06 (commit `d3965c8a`) with content hash
`5ec8b5a46cf5cbeb7a36be14613288a88a3eea34612ec5d13926c6095caab742`. Later the same day the macOS
account was renamed `gduby` → `och`. Every absolute path into the old home stopped resolving, including
the corpus root recorded inside the declaration.

Because `declaration()` hashes that root, the dead path could not be corrected without changing the
hash. It was corrected, and both hashes are recorded here.

## Why this is not a post-hoc widening

The freeze exists to stop the search space widening after a result looks attractive — the lesson of
ledger row 183. That risk is absent here, and the absence is checkable rather than asserted:

1. **No economic outcome had been inspected.** The order of work in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) puts the surrogate
   known-answer campaign (step 4) before any real economics, and steps 3–7 are still outstanding. No
   member's profit and loss has been computed at any point.
2. **The diff is exactly one string, and it is not a research quantity.** A machine path is not a
   mechanism, direction, horizon, occupancy rule, clock, comparator, fold rule, or null. All 18 members,
   all 6 pass criteria, and all surrogate gates are byte-identical.
3. **The old hash reproduces on demand.** Substituting the old root back into the current declaration
   returns `5ec8b5a4…` exactly, which proves no second change is hiding inside the reissue.
4. **The corpus is the same corpus.** 261 files, and the two integration tests reproduce the frozen
   eligible index from the bars themselves — 254 M1-eligible sessions, 249 gap-eligible, and the four
   contract rolls on exactly the declared dates.

## How the discrepancy surfaced

It surfaced as a false green. After the rename, the two tests that read the real corpus were skipping on
`Path(family.ES_BARS_ROOT).is_dir()` and the suite still reported success — 142 passed, 3 skipped, with
the skip reasons ("owned ES bars not present", "owned corpus not mounted") visible only under `-rs`.

That is the more general lesson worth keeping: **an absolute path inside a skip condition converts a
broken environment into a passing test run.** The corpus checks in
[`test_reservation.py`](../../tests/test_reservation.py) and the G1 loader tests are now home-relative,
and the suite reports **145 passed, 0 skipped**.
